Engineering principles

Principle 01

Zero Manual Work

Recipes and Blender scenes are baked headless, packed, QA-gated and released by scripts. A full re-bake is one command — not a week of exporting frames by hand.

Principle 02

Zero-GC / A15 Verified 60–120 FPS

Steady-state playback allocates under 100 B/frame with no major GC — measured with 50–100 concurrent casts at 60 and 120 FPS loops. Every asset's decoded VRAM is checked against the iPhone 13 mini (A15) budget of 45 MB per direction: {{mobileTier}} of {{vfxCount}} effects pass and carry the A15 badge; the {{heavyTier}} heavier ones ship as Desktop Tier.
