Engineering principles

Principle 01

Zero Manual Work

Recipes and Blender scenes are baked headless, packed, QA-gated and released by scripts. A full re-bake is one command — not a week of exporting frames by hand.

Principle 02

Zero-GC / A15 Budget 60–120 FPS

Steady-state playback allocates under 100 B/frame with no major GC — measured with 50–100 concurrent casts at 60 and 120 FPS. Mobile packs are sized for an iPhone 13 mini (A15): 45 MB VRAM per direction.
